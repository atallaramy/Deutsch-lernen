# Zahlen: numbers 0–100

*die Zahl* (number) · A1 · from VHS A1 Lektion 2 (and the DW examples below) · checked 2026-09-26

## The rule

1. **0–12**: learn them by heart. *elf* and *zwölf* are irregular.
2. **13–19**: the single digit + *zehn*: *dreizehn*. In 16 and 17 the digit loses its
   ending: *sechzehn*, *siebzehn*.
3. **Tens**: digit + *-zig*: *vierzig*. Irregular: *zehn*, *zwanzig*, *dreißig* (with ß),
   *sechzig*, *siebzig*.
4. **21–99**: digit + **und** + ten, written as one word: *dreiundzwanzig*.
   *eins* loses its -s: *einundzwanzig*.
5. **100**: *hundert* or *einhundert*.

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| fünfzehn, siebzehn | fünfzehn, sechzehn, siebzehn | *sechzehn* is missing from my list. It is the tricky one: *sechs* loses its -s. | [my notes: VHS Lektion 2](<../../VHS-Lernportal/A1/Lesson_02/lektion_02.md>) |
| sechszehn | sechzehn | 16 drops the -s of *sechs*. | rule |
| siebenzehn | siebzehn | 17 drops the -en of *sieben*. | rule |
| dreizig | dreißig | 30 is written with ß. | rule |
| eins und zwanzig | einundzwanzig | One word, and *eins* becomes *ein-*. | rule |
