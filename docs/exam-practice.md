# Exam practice (when you say "exam practice")

Practice for the **Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1**, using only the exam
parts whose topics you have already studied. It runs only when you ask. It never
builds cards and sets no deadline. Exam rules, dates and fees: [links.md](links.md)
→ *Exams and levels*.

## Steps (Claude)

1. Find the studied lessons: the lessons in `ANKI/lesson-vocabulary.json`, plus
   any lesson you say you have studied since then.
2. Read the log below. Pick the next one or two parts from the map whose topics
   are all studied, in this order: unused parts first, then the part with the
   lowest score.
3. Give at most 5 lines: which set, which pages to print, what to do, the time
   limit, where the answers are, and what to send to Claude.
4. Mark what you send (writing text, speaking text or answers) with the official
   marking pages of that set, as a Goethe examiner would: explain with Goethe's
   criteria and Goethe's own materials, not with the course lessons. Show the score and the wrong words exactly as you
   wrote them. You fix them once. Then Claude adds one line to the log.
   Speaking sent as dictation text is marked only on what can be heard (words,
   forms, word order), never on capitals or punctuation.
5. Full timed tests are given only when the map says so. Keep at least one set
   untouched for the last 1–2 weeks before a booked exam. Give no new tests in
   the last 3 days; only go over the earlier mistakes.

## Map: exam part → what you need to have studied

The topics come from the sets themselves. DW/VHS columns name the first lessons
that cover them ([course-index/](course-index/)).

| Part | Task | Needs | DW Nicos Weg A1 | VHS A1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Schreiben Teil 1 | Fill 5 gaps in a form from a short text | name, numbers, address, persons | E1–E2 | L1–L2 |
| Sprechen Teil 1 | Introduce yourself (*Name, Alter, Land, Wohnort, Sprachen, Beruf, Hobby*), spell, say a number | the same, plus one learned sentence each for job and hobby | E1–E2 | L1–L2 |
| Sprechen Teil 2 | Ask and answer about a topic word on a card | W-questions, everyday topics (food, shopping, free time) | E2 L3 + E3 | L1 + L4 |
| Hören Teil 1 | 6 short dialogues, choose a/b/c | numbers, prices, times, days, places in a building | E2–E3, E7–E8 | L2–L5 |
| Hören Teil 3 | 5 phone messages, choose a/b/c | phone numbers, times, meeting places | E7–E8 | L5 |
| Lesen Teil 3 | 5 signs and notices, true/false | opening times, days, places | E7–E8 | L5, L7 |
| Lesen Teil 1 | 2 short messages, true/false | times, travel, plans | E7–E8, E10 | L5, L7 |
| Hören Teil 2 | 4 announcements, true/false, heard once | times, platforms, directions | E8, E10 | L5, L7 |
| Lesen Teil 2 | Pick the right ad or website (a/b) | travel, shopping, weather, services | E10, E12–E13 | L7, L11–L12 |
| Schreiben Teil 2 | Short message, ~30 words, 3 points, greeting and sign-off | present tense, *möchten*, requests, dates | E8–E9 | L8 |
| Sprechen Teil 3 | Make a request from a picture card and react | *Können Sie bitte …?*, imperative | E8 L3, E12 L4 | L7–L8 |
| **Full timed test** | All parts, 65 min written + speaking | about half the course, with requests | after E8 | after L8 |
