# Goethe A1 practice materials

For the **Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1** exam. A *Modellsatz* ("model set") is Goethe's
official sample exam: all four parts with listening audio, transcripts, answers and marking
guides. An *Übungssatz* ("practice set") is the same with other questions. Say "exam practice"
to get the part that fits what you have studied. Checked 2026-10-01.

## When to start: small parts first, the full test after DW unit 8

| When | What to do | What to print |
|---|---|---|
| Now (after DW unit 2) | Writing part 1 (the form) and speaking part 1 (introduce yourself), about 15 minutes | Modellsatz pages 24 and 28 only |
| After DW unit 3 | Speaking part 2 (ask and answer about a topic) | Modellsatz pages 29–30 |
| After DW unit 8 | The first full timed test | The rest of the Modellsatz: pages 7–32 and 43–44 |

Say "exam practice" after a lesson; Claude names the part and the pages each time.
