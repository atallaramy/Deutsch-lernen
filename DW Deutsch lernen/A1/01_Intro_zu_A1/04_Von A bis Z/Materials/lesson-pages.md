## Culture: How's it going?

<https://learngerman.dw.com/en/hows-it-going/l-37256418/rs-40965828>

The question "Wie geht es dir?" ("How are you?" – informal) or "Wie geht es Ihnen?" ("How are you?" – formal) often comes immediately after a greeting. It is a standard part of the conversation, as it is considered polite to ask someone how they are doing or feeling. But be careful: sometimes people are not really interested in the answer. In a conversation with someone you don't know that well, your answer should be very brief, for example, "Danke, gut" ("Very well, thanks)".

But if a friend asks you the question, you may respond honestly and in detail, since that person really wants to know how you are.

## Culture: The phonetic alphabet

<https://learngerman.dw.com/en/the-phonetic-alphabet/l-37256418/rs-39371795>

In German, the alphabet letters with city names have only been in use since 2022. The previous alphabet letter system was primarily made up of first names. Many people still use it today.
