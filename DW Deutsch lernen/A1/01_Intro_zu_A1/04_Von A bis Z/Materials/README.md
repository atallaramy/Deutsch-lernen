# Von A bis Z — official DW materials

These are the distinct English-accessible materials DW publishes for the
*Nicos Weg A1* lesson **Von A bis Z**. They are saved locally for browsing;
their original DW URLs are included below.

- `Von A bis Z — Script and vocabulary (English).pdf` — dialogue transcript
  and English vocabulary list. Original:
  <https://static.dw.com/downloads/52719090/nicos-weg-a1-e0-l4-manuskript-und-wortschatz-englisch.pdf>
- `Von A bis Z — Teacher guide and exercises (German).pdf` — lesson exercises
  and teaching notes. Original:
  <https://static.dw.com/downloads/52719092/nicos-weg-a1-e0-l4-lehrerhandreichung-und-uebungen.pdf>

This folder intentionally excludes duplicate editions in other interface
languages and materials for other lessons.

## Pronunciation audio

`Phonetic alphabet audio/` contains offline German text-to-speech MP3s of DW's
phonetic-alphabet list. The individual files are packaged in
`Von A bis Z_Phonetic Alphabet_Pronunciation.apkg`; the combined
`Phonetisches Alphabet — pronunciation.mp3` is a standalone file for Files on
Mac and iPhone.
