| Table Name | Meaning | When to Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indicative (*Indikativ*)** | Describes real, actual actions, facts, and events across all timeframes. | Statements about reality, facts, routine actions, or past/future occurrences. |
| **Subjunctive (*Konjunktiv*)** | Represents hypothetical, non-real, potential, or reported situations. | Expressing wishes, conditional ("what-if") scenarios, or reporting indirect speech. |
| **Conditional (*würde*-Form)** | Uses the auxiliary verb *würde* with an infinitive to express conditions. | Modern spoken alternative to Subjunctive II for hypothetical actions or polite requests. |
| **Imperative (*Imperativ*)** | Expresses direct commands, requests, or instructions. | Giving orders, directions, advice, or making direct requests to someone. |
| **Infinitive / Participle** | Non-finite verb forms that do not change for person or number. | Linking verbs together, forming compound tenses, or using verbs as adjectives/nouns. |
