-   Ein Bücherregal ist ein Regal.
    "Bücherregal" is a compound word.
    "Regal" is the root word.
    The root word at the end of the compound determines:
    1. gender: masculine, feminine, or neuter
    2. number: singular or plural
    die Bücher + das Regal = das Bücherregal

- die Bücher + das Regal = das Bücherregal
- der Spiegel + der Schrank = der Spiegelschrank
- das Büro + der Stuhl = der Bürostuhl

Extra tasks

- Watch the film again and write down every comment Wolfgang makes about the living room and the furniture (e.g. Der Tisch, der ist nicht schön.).

- Write a short profile (Steckbrief) of Wolfgang: Alter, Beruf, Wohnort, Hobbys. Guess what the film doesn't tell you.

- Which living room do you like better: the room as it is now, or Wolfgang's room for chilling and jamming? Write 3–4 sentences and say why, with zu + adjective (e.g. Der Schrank ist viel zu groß.).

- Describe your own living room in 5–6 sentences: which furniture you have (der/die/das!), what it is like, and one thing that is too … .

- Role-play with Claude: Claude asks about your furniture (Hast du einen Teppich? Hast du …?) and you answer; then you ask Claude.
