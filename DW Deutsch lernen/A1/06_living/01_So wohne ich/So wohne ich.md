Extra tasks

- Watch the film once without the script. Write 4–5 short sentences about what happens: Wo wohnt Nico? Was ist das Problem? Was macht Lisa?

- Where should Nico sleep: in a hotel, in a youth hostel or in a room with friends? Write 3–4 sentences, e.g. Ein Hotel ist sehr teuer. Eine Jugendherberge ist nicht so teuer wie ein Hotel.

- Learn the dialogue between Lisa and Nico by heart and say it aloud. Then move it to a café or to the phone: write it again with 2 new lines (e.g. Möchtest du noch einen Kaffee?).

- Role-play with Claude: Claude is Lisa, you are Nico. Lisa tells you the problem; you ask questions and decide where you sleep.
