Extra tasks

- Watch the film twice without subtitles. First time: which rooms do they talk about? Second time: which adjectives do you hear, and which room does each one describe? (e.g. Die Küche ist sehr gemütlich.)

- Draw a floor plan of your flat (or of a dream flat) and describe it in 5–6 sentences: the rooms, how big, what they are like, and one sentence with nicht, e.g. Die Küche ist nicht groß, sie ist klein.

- Marvin has left. Write a short dialogue (6–8 lines): Lisa, Nawin and Sebastian talk about Marvin. Is he nice? Does he get the room?

- Role-play with Claude: a WG viewing. Claude shows you the flat; you are the Interessent or Interessentin and ask about the rooms, the square meters and the rent (Wie viele Zimmer …? Wie teuer …?).
