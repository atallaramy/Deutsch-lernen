Extra tasks

- Write your own week like Emma: one sentence for each day with am + day, and at least two separable verbs (e.g. Am Montag spiele ich Fußball. Am Samstag stehe ich spät auf.).

- Draw a calendar for next week with days and times and write 4–5 sentences about it with am, um and von … bis (e.g. Am Mittwochabend koche ich um sieben.).

- Write a short dialogue like Nawin and Nina: invite a friend (Ich gehe am Freitag ins Café. Kommst du mit?), the friend asks Wann denn?, says Das geht nicht. and suggests another day.

- Role-play with Claude: Claude asks Was machst du am …? or Kochst du auch am Montagabend?; you answer, e.g. Nein, ich koche am Mittwochabend. / Ja, ich koche auch am Montagabend.

- Many Germans plan their free time. Write 3–4 short sentences about free time in your country: what do children or adults do, and on which days?
