- Seprable prefixes are, for example, "auf", "aus", "mit", "ein".
- Careful! Words like "und", "oder" and "aber" do not count when determining word positions. Therefore the verb is also in position two.
- Tip: When you learn verbs, you should learn right away whether or not they are separable. When you write them out, you can put a line between the prefix and the verb stem: auf|räumen.

Reference:
- Verbs with certain prefixes, for example: ab-, an-, auf-, aus-, ein-, mit-, nach-, weg-, zu-  are always separable.
- In contrast, verbs with certain prefixes, for example:  be-, ent-, er-, ver-, zer- are not separable.
- Verbs with prefixes like durch-, über-, um-, unter- can be separable or inseparable.
