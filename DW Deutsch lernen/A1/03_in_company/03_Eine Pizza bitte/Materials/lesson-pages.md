## Grammar: Vowel change: e to i

<https://learngerman.dw.com/en/vowel-change-e-to-i/l-37279261/gr-38306672>

**Verbs with a vowel change from** ***e*** **to** ***i***

With some irregular verbs, the root vowel *e* changes. It becomes *i* in the second- and third-person singular. The other verb forms remain regular.

*Ich **esse** gern Pizza mit Salami. Und was **isst** du gern, Nina? – Ich **esse** gern Pizza Hawaii ohne Schinken.*

|                              |                    |
|------------------------------|--------------------|
| Singular                     |                    |
| 1st person                   | ich esse           |
| 2nd person                   | du **isst**        |
| 3rd person                   | er/sie/es **isst** |
| Plural                       |                    |
| 1st person                   | wir essen          |
| 2nd person                   | ihr esst           |
| 3rd person                   | sie essen          |
| Formal (singular and plural) | Sie essen          |

 

***The verb nehmen***

The verb *nehmen* also belongs to this group. It has another irregularity:

In the second- and third-person singular, it not only changes the vowel, but also the following letter.

*Ich **nehme** eine Pizza mit Thunfisch. Und was **nimmst** du, Nico? – Ich **nehme** eine Pizza mit Salami.*

|                              |                     |
|------------------------------|---------------------|
| Singular                     |                     |
| 1st person                   | ich nehme           |
| 2nd person                   | du **nimmst**       |
| 3rd person                   | er/sie/es **nimmt** |
| Plural                       |                     |
| 1st person                   | wir nehmen          |
| 2nd person                   | ihr nehmt           |
| 3rd person                   | sie nehmen          |
| Formal (singular and plural) | Sie nehmen          |

## Culture: Pizza, fries and sausages

<https://learngerman.dw.com/en/pizza-fries-and-sausages/l-37279261/rs-40966522>

Wurst, pizza and kebab - Filling meals that you can eat quickly on the go are popular in Germany, too. So snack bars are well-liked. There are snack bars everywhere, not just in the big cities. Diverse menus are available, though the meals are not always healthy. But they are delicious. You'll find typical German dishes, like bratwurst or grilled chicken with bread rolls or French fries. But food from other cultures has long found its place in fast food culture in Germany. Pizza, doner kebab or burgers are just as popular as German sausages. In addition to small snack bars, there are also well-known international fast food chains in Germany.

## Culture: Regional or international?

<https://learngerman.dw.com/en/regional-or-international/l-37279261/rs-39371005>

If you think you will just be eating local foods like potatoes, sausages and sauerkraut in Germany, then you are wrong. As well as regional specialities, people in Germany love international cuisine. Particularly in big cities, you find restaurants offering foreign food.

Lots of Germans also like trying to cook international dishes at home. Supermarkets have adapted to accommodate this trend. You can get specialist ingredients in shops like Asian and Turkish grocery stores.
