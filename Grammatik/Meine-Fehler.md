## How this list grows

At every Round 1 Claude compares the new mistakes with this list. A repeat raises its
*Times* and gets one line under the Round 1 table (*Again: is → ist, 7×*). A mistake
that reaches 3× joins the list. It also gets one Sentences card that practises it, unless typed
cards already test it. All the mistakes above are already tested that way: every answer is typed
strictly (capitals, letters, endings, punctuation).
