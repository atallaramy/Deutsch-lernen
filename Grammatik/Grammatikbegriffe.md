# Grammatikbegriffe: grammar words

Every German grammar word from my studied lessons · checked 2026-10-07

Words that mean the same share a row, split by ` / `. The page explains the word
with examples from my lessons. The same words, with a short example each, are on my
one-page cheatsheet `Spickzettel/Grammatikbegriffe.pdf`.
