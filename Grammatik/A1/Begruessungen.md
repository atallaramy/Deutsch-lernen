# Begrüßungen und Verabschiedungen: who says what, when

*die Begrüßung* (greeting) · *die Verabschiedung* (goodbye) · A1 · from DW A1 E0 L1 *Hallo!*, E0 L3 *Tschüss!*, E0 L4 *Von A bis Z* and Easy German SEG 274 · checked 2026-09-26

## The rule

1. Formal (Sie, *Herr/Frau* + surname): *Guten Tag*, *Auf Wiedersehen*.
   Informal (du, first name): *Hallo*, *Tschüss*. Not sure? Be formal.
2. By the clock (DW): *Guten Morgen* until about 11 a.m., *Guten Tag* from about
   11 a.m. to 6 p.m., *Guten Abend* from about 6 p.m.
3. After hello comes *Wie geht's?* / *Wie geht es Ihnen?* With someone you don't
   know well, answer briefly: *Danke, gut.* (DW culture page *How's it going?*)
4. The greetings themselves are Vocabulary cards. This page is for choosing the right one.

Hello:

| Say | Register | Who · when · where | Checked in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hallo! · Hi! · Hey! | informal · everyday | friends, acquaintances, young people | DW culture page *Greetings in German* |

| Guten Morgen! | formal | until about 11 a.m. | DW *Informal and formal (1)* |
| Guten Tag! | formal | the most important formal greeting, all day | DW culture page and *Informal and formal (1)* |
| Guten Abend! | formal | from about 6 p.m. | DW *Informal and formal (1)* |
| Moin! · Moinsen! | colloquial | Northern Germany; *Moinsen* is slang | DW culture page; Wiktionary |
| Servus! | informal | especially Bavaria and Austria; also means bye | DW culture pages |
| Grüß Gott! | — | Southern Germany and Austria | DW culture page |
| Grüezi! | polite | Switzerland | DW culture page; Wiktionary |
| Habe die Ehre! | polite | Austria, Bavaria | Wiktionary |

Goodbye:

| Say | Register | Who · when · where | Checked in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Auf Wiedersehen! | formal | strangers, business, officials, shops | DW *Informal and formal (2)* |
| Auf Wiederhören! | formal | on the phone | DW culture page *Saying goodbye* |
| Schönen Tag noch! | polite | especially in shops (DW: *Einen schönen Tag noch!*) | DW culture page |
| Tschüss! | informal | the most common goodbye, used all over Germany | DW culture page |
| Ciao! | informal | popular with young people | DW culture page |
| Mach's gut! | informal | relaxed; wishes the other person luck | DW culture page |
| Bis bald! | informal | when you will see the person again soon | DW culture page |
| Bis später! · Bis dann! | — | see you later / see you then | Wiktionary |
| Bis die Tage! · Man sieht sich! | casual (*salopp*) | see you some time soon | DWDS |
| Pfiat di! · Baba! | informal | *Pfiat di*: mainly Austria and Bavaria; *Baba*: Austria | Wiktionary |
