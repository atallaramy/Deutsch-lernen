# Ja-/Nein-Fragen and W-Fragen

*die Ja-/Nein-Frage* (yes/no question) · A1 · from VHS A1 Lektion 2, DW A1 E0 L3 to E1 L4 and E4 L2 *Wo liegt das?* · checked 2026-10-03

## The rule

German terms:
- *die Ja-/Nein-Frage* / *die Satzfrage* (yes/no question: This is a question that can be answered with "yes" or "no".)
- *die W-Frage* / *die Ergänzungsfrage* (W-question: W-questions are open questions that cannot be answered with "yes" or "no". They are also called *Ergänzungsfragen*. At the beginning of a W-question there is a question word that usually begins with the letter *W*.)
- *der Aussagesatz* (statement: In a statement, a fact is declared. The statement may give information or describe a situation.)

VHS says *Ja-/Nein-Frage*; DW says *Satzfrage*.

1. A yes/no question starts with the **verb**, then the subject: *Hast du einen Pass?*
   The answer can start with *ja* or *nein*.
2. A W-question starts with the **question word**, then the verb: *Wo wohnst du?*
   It asks for information, so *ja* or *nein* is not an answer.
3. In a statement the verb is in **second place**: *Ich wohne in Sevilla.*
4. In speech a statement can be said as a question. In the DW script Max checks
   Nico's answer this way: *Du wohnst in Sevilla?*
5. *…, oder?* at the end asks "isn't that right?": *Du kommst nicht aus Deutschland, oder?*

The word order is from DW's grammar page
[Questions and statements](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/questions-and-statements/l-37337877/gr-38306265)
(E4 L2).

|---|---|---|---|

| Ja-/Nein-Frage | Wohnst | du | in Barcelona oder in Madrid? |

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wie viele Kinder haben Sie. | Wie viele Kinder haben Sie? | A question ends with **?**. Typed answers are strict about it. | [my notes: VHS Lektion 2](<../../VHS-Lernportal/A1/Lesson_02/lektion_02.md>) |
| Wo wohnst du? – Ja. | Wo wohnst du? – Ich wohne in Sevilla. | A W-question needs information, not *ja*. | rule |
