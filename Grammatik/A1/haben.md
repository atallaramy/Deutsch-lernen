# haben: to have

*das Verb haben* · irregular in two forms · A1 · from DW A1 E1 L4 *Nico hat ein Problem*, DW A1 E2 L1, E3 L2 and VHS A1 Lektion 2 · checked 2026-10-02

## The rule

1. *haben* drops the **b** in two forms: *du hast*, *er/sie/es hat*.
2. All the other forms are regular: *hab-* + ending (*ich habe, wir haben, ihr habt*).
3. In *Hast du einen Pass?* the thing you have changes its article: *der Pass* →
   *einen Pass*. That is the accusative, which comes later with the cases.

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| du habst | du hast | The b drops out for du. | rule |
| er habt | er hat | The b drops out for er/sie/es. *habt* is the ihr form. | rule |
| ihr hat | ihr habt | ihr is regular: *hab-* + *-t*. | rule |
