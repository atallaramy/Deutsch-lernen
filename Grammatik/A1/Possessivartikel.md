# Possessivartikel: mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euer, Ihr

*der Possessivartikel* (possessive determiner) · A1 · from VHS A1 Lektion 2 and DW A1 E0 L4 *Von A bis Z*, E1 L1 *Ich heiße Emma*, E1 L3 *Woher kommst du?*, E2 L2 *Wichtige Nummern*, E6 L4 *Unser Haus* · checked 2026-10-20

## The rule

German terms:
- *der Possessivartikel* (possessive determiner: The possessive determiner is an article word that expresses ownership or belonging.)

1. A possessive determiner says whose something is. It stands where *der/die/das* or
   *ein/eine* would stand: *das Zimmer → mein Zimmer*.
   *mein* = my, *dein* = your (informal, du), *sein* = his/its, *ihr* = her, *unser* = our,
   *euer* = your (ihr, you all), *ihr* = their, *Ihr* = your (formal, Sie).
   *Ihr* always has a capital I, like *Sie*. Small *ihr* means her or their.
   At the start of a sentence *Ihr* can also be *their*: *Ihr Kinderzimmer ist hier.* (Josi and Livi's)
2. **sein** or **ihr**? Look at the owner: a man's (or a *der/das* owner's) → *sein*, a woman's → *ihr*:
   *Das ist das Zimmer von Sebastian. Das ist sein Zimmer.* · *Das ist das Zimmer von Lisa. Das ist ihr Zimmer.*
   The ending depends on the **noun that follows**, not on the owner.
3. Here, before the subject of the sentence (nominative):
   - *der* noun or *das* noun: no ending: *mein Vater*, *mein Telefon*
   - *die* noun or a plural: <nom>-e</nom>: *mein<nom>e</nom> Tante*, *mein<nom>e</nom> Eltern*
4. *euer* + <nom>-e</nom> drops its second *e*: *euer Wohnzimmer*, but *eur<nom>e</nom> Kinder*.
5. Other cases (*meinen*, *meinem* …) come later with the cases.

## Table

| | der noun | das noun | die noun | plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ich → | mein | mein | mein<nom>e</nom> | mein<nom>e</nom> |
| du → | dein | dein | dein<nom>e</nom> | dein<nom>e</nom> |
| er/es → | sein | sein | sein<nom>e</nom> | sein<nom>e</nom> |
| sie → | ihr | ihr | ihr<nom>e</nom> | ihr<nom>e</nom> |
| wir → | unser | unser | unser<nom>e</nom> | unser<nom>e</nom> |

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| meine Vater | mein Vater | *der Vater*: no ending. | rule |
| mein Tante | meine Tante | *die Tante*: add <nom>-e</nom>. | rule |
| ihr Name (to a stranger) | Ihr Name | Formal *Ihr* has a capital I. Small *ihr* means her or their. | rule |
| ihr Zimmer (Sebastian's) | sein Zimmer | A man's → *sein*; a woman's → *ihr*. | rule |
| euere Kinder | eure Kinder | *euer* + *-e* → *eure*. | rule |
