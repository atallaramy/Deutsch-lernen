# man: people in general

*das Indefinitpronomen* (indefinite pronoun) · A1 · from DW A1 E4 L3 *In Europa* · checked 2026-10-04

## The rule

German terms:
- *das Indefinitpronomen* (indefinite pronoun: Indefinite pronouns refer to people or things that are indeterminate. They are used when one does not know, or does not wish to say exactly who, or how many, are doing something.)

1. **man** makes a general statement. It means people in general (a whole group,
   or everyone), not one particular person: *In Frankreich isst man gern Käse.*
2. *man* is always **singular**, even when it means many people. The verb takes
   the **er/sie/es** form: *er isst* → *man isst*.
3. *man* can come first or right after the verb: *Man isst in Spanien gern Gazpacho.*
   · *In Japan isst man Sushi.* The verb stays in position 2 ([Aussagesatz](Aussagesatz.md)).
4. **man** (one *n*, small letter) means people in general. **der Mann** (*nn*,
   capital) is a man, a male person.

Source: DW grammar page [Indefinite pronouns: man](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/indefinite-pronouns-man/l-37328492/gr-38306317) (E4 L3);
DW exercise [The use of "man" (2)](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/the-use-of-man-2/l-37328492/e-37337538)
(with *man* the verb is in the third-person singular).

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| In Japan essen man Sushi. | In Japan isst man Sushi. | *man* is singular: the er/sie/es form. | rule |
| Mann isst in Italien Pizza. | Man isst in Italien Pizza. | *man* (one *n*) = people; *der Mann* = a man. | rule |
