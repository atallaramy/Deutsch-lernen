# Präteritum: war (sein)

*das Präteritum* (simple past) · A1 · from DW A1 E4 L1 *Ich war schon in Berlin* · checked 2026-10-03

## The rule

German terms:
- *das Präteritum* (simple past: The simple past is a verb tense. It is used in written language to describe events, activities or situations that happened in the past. The simple past of certain verbs, e.g. *sein*, is often also used in spoken language.)
- *das Präsens* (present tense: The present is a verb tense. It is used when speaking about events, activities or situations that happen in the present or are continuing to happen.)

1. The Präteritum is a past tense. DW: it is mostly used in writing, but *sein* and
   *haben* are often used in the Präteritum when speaking too.
2. *sein* in the Präteritum has the stem **war**:
   - ich and er/sie/es: just **war**, no ending.
   - du **warst** · ihr **wart** · wir, sie and Sie **waren**.
3. Been somewhere before: **Ich war schon mal in …** (*schon mal* is short for *schon einmal*).
   Never been there: **Ich war noch nie in …**. To ask: **Warst du schon mal in …?**
4. A town or country after *war* takes **in**, as with *wohnen* ([wohnen in](wohnen-in.md)).

Source: DW grammar page [Simple past: sein](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/simple-past-sein/l-37325550/gr-38306053) (E4 L1);
DW exercise tip [Where has Nawin already been?](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/where-has-nawin-already-been/l-37325550/e-37328550) (*schon mal* / *noch nie*).

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ihr war | Ihr wart | *ihr* has its own form: **wart** (like *ihr seid*). | [my mistakes: Ich war schon in Berlin](<../../DW Deutsch lernen/A1/05_around_the_world/01_Lesson_01/Materials/mistakes.md>) |
| War | Warst | *du* → **warst**: *Warst du schon mal in München?* | [my mistakes: Ich war schon in Berlin](<../../DW Deutsch lernen/A1/05_around_the_world/01_Lesson_01/Materials/mistakes.md>) |
| ihr waren | ihr wart | Only wir, sie and Sie take *waren*. | rule |
| er warst | er war | er/sie/es is like ich: **war**, no ending. | rule |
