# Plural: ein Stift, zwei Stifte

*der Plural* · A1 · from DW A1 E5 L2 *Wem gehört das?* · checked 2026-10-05

## The rule

German terms:
- *der Singular* (singular: The singular is used when we are talking about one individual person or thing.)
- *der Plural* (plural: The plural is used when we are talking about several people or things.)
- *die Endung* (ending: In conjugation, the endings of the verbs often change. The ending depends on which person is doing the action. The ending comes after the verb stem.)
- *der Umlaut* (umlaut: "Ä", "Ö" and "Ü" are the three letters in German with umlauts.)

1. German has five plural endings: **-n, -en, -e, -er, -s**. Some nouns add no ending.
2. Some nouns also get an umlaut: *das Buch → die Bücher*, *der Notizblock → die Notizblöcke*.
3. There is no dependable rule, so learn every noun with its article and its plural:
   *der Stift, die Stifte*. A dictionary gives the plural, often only as an ending (*-e*).
4. Every plural takes **die**, and the verb is plural too: *Die Stifte **kosten** 7,50 Euro.*
5. The plural has no indefinite article: *Das sind Stifte.* ([Unbestimmter Artikel](Unbestimmter-Artikel.md))
6. Some nouns have two plurals, both right: *die Pizza, die Pizzen/die Pizzas*.

Source: DW grammar page [Nouns: plural](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/nouns-plural/l-37372077/gr-38306003) (E5 L2).

## Table

| Ending | Example |
|---|---|
| -n | die Brille, die Brillen |
| -en | die Zahl, die Zahlen |
| -e | der Stift, die Stifte |
| -er | das Kind, die Kinder |
| -s | das Foto, die Fotos |
| no ending | der Lehrer, die Lehrer |
| umlaut + ending | das Buch, die Bücher · der Notizblock, die Notizblöcke |
| umlaut, no ending | der Flughafen, die Flughäfen |
