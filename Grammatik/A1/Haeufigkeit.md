# Häufigkeit: immer, oft, manchmal, selten, nie

*das Adverb* (adverb) · A1 · from DW A1 E7 L2 *Tageszeiten* · checked 2026-10-20

## The rule

German terms:
- *das Adverb* (adverb: An adverb describes when, where or how something happens. It refers to another word in the sentence (e.g. a verb or adjective) or to the whole sentence.)

1. To say **how often** you do something, use an adverb of time, e.g. *immer*, *nie*.
2. From most often to never (DW): *immer* · *fast immer* · *oft* · *manchmal* ·
   *selten* · *fast nie* · *nie*.
3. These adverbs usually come **right after the conjugated verb**:
   *Emma macht **immer** die Hausaufgaben.*
4. One can also come first; then the verb follows it in position 2:
   ***Manchmal** spielen Lisa und ich Fußball.* ([Aussagesatz](Aussagesatz.md))
5. More words from the lesson: *normalerweise* (normally), *meistens* (most of the
   time; short form *meist*), *jeden Tag* (every day).

Source: DW grammar page [Adverbs: frequency (1)](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/adverbs-frequency-1/l-37445884/gr-38304940) (E7 L2)
and the exercise [Always or almost always? (2)](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/always-or-almost-always-2/l-37445884/e-37454892) (the order).
