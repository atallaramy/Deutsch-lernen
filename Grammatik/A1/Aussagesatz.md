# Aussagesatz: the verb in position 2

*der Aussagesatz* (statement) · A1 · from DW A1 E4 L3 *In Europa* · checked 2026-10-04

## The rule

German terms: *der Aussagesatz* (statement), *das Verb* (verb), *das Subjekt* (subject).

1. In a statement the **conjugated verb** (the verb with its ending) is always in
   **position 2**.
2. The subject often comes first: *Ich bin jetzt in Deutschland.*
3. To stress another part, put it first. Often this is a time (*jetzt*, *heute*)
   or a place (*in Frankreich*), sometimes another part (*Pizza*). The verb stays
   in position 2, and the **subject moves to position 3**, right after the verb.
4. Words that belong together fill one position: *In Frankreich* | *isst* | *man* …
   Position 2 is the second part, not always the second word.

Source: DW grammar page [Sentence construction: Statements](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/sentence-construction-statements/l-37328492/gr-38306661) (E4 L3);
DW exercise tip [Where does one eat what?](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/where-does-one-eat-what/l-37328492/e-37337312)
(*Sushi isst man in Japan* stresses *Sushi*).

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jetzt ich bin in Deutschland. | Jetzt bin ich in Deutschland. | The verb stays in position 2; the subject comes after it. | rule |
| Heute wir besichtigen den Dom. | Heute besichtigen wir den Dom. | English says "Today we visit"; German puts the verb before the subject. | rule |
| In Frankreich man isst viel Käse. | In Frankreich isst man viel Käse. | *In Frankreich* is one part, so *isst* comes next. | rule |
